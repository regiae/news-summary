Russian and Ukrainian negotiators opened a second day of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi on Wednesday, their first direct talks since February, Reuters reported. Marco Rubio called day one very productive, but the sides stayed far apart.

Brent crude rose 0.8% to $92.20 as attacks on shipping in the Strait of Hormuz continued, Reuters reported. The Washington Institute counted 37 ships attacked there in October, a monthly record, and 10 more in November.

Congo's Ebola outbreak passed 11,000 cases, the Associated Press reported: 11,042 infected and 5,387 dead. The Bundibugyo strain, with no vaccine or treatment, has reached eight of its 26 provinces.

World leaders began two days of speeches at COP31 in Antalya on Wednesday, Reuters reported, as nearly 200 countries seek an electricity and fossil-fuel deal. UN chief Antonio Guterres urged clear timelines on Tuesday.

TSMC will invest up to $500 million each in Anthropic and OpenAI, Morningstar reported, after its board approved them. Anthropic set its IPO range at $70 to $78 a share to raise over $61 billion.

Asian shares rebounded on Wednesday, the Associated Press reported, with Tokyo's Nikkei 225 up 0.8% to about 60,940 and Seoul's Kospi up 2.6% to about 5,375. The Kosdaq jumped about 5%.

The 990-foot SS United States went down off Destin, Florida, the Pensacola News Journal reported, becoming the world's largest artificial reef. The sinking came a day early over forecast winds, and scientists will study its new marine life.

A city bus plowed into 32 vehicles at a traffic light in Seongnam, South Korea, on Wednesday morning, Yonhap reported, injuring 57 people, nine seriously. The driver, in his 60s, blamed failed brakes.

Bulgaria held a day of mourning for the 12 killed in a pile-up of about 86 vehicles in dense fog on the Trakia motorway, the Associated Press reported. Around 50 more were injured near Yambol.
