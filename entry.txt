Russia and Ukraine agreed a large-scale prisoner exchange as US-mediated talks ended in Abu Dhabi on Wednesday, Reuters reported. Volodymyr Zelenskyy said 1,000 or possibly 1,500 prisoners per side were under discussion.

Brent crude rose 0.6% to $92.01 a barrel as Hormuz shipping attacks continued, Reuters reported. A tanker was struck northeast of Lima in Oman on Tuesday; the Washington Institute counted 37 ships attacked there in October and 10 more in November.

Mohsen Rezaei, Iran's security chief, said Tehran awaited a US answer to its seven conditions and the Strait of Hormuz would stay shut until they were met. Secretary of State Marco Rubio said economic pressure made major combat operations unnecessary, the New York Times reported.

Congo's Ebola outbreak reached 11,042 cases and 5,387 deaths, the Associated Press reported, the second-largest and second-deadliest on record. The Bundibugyo strain has reached eight provinces, and Nigeria confirmed an imported case in Abuja.

World leaders opened a two-day summit at COP31 in Antalya on Wednesday, Reuters reported, with 102 heads of state and about 300 ministers expected. UN chief Antonio Guterres warned the slow response to climate change had put the world on a path to destruction.

TSMC's board approved up to $500 million each in Anthropic and OpenAI, Morningstar reported. Anthropic set its IPO price range at $70 to $78 a share, valuing it at up to $1.4 trillion, with pricing on November 18.

Malaysia's ex-prime minister Najib Razak arrived home to serve the rest of his sentence under house arrest, Reuters reported. The High Court cleared the transfer after four years in prison; he stays home until August 23, 2028, then serves a separate 15-year 1MDB sentence.

Asian shares rebounded, the Associated Press reported, with Tokyo's Nikkei 225 at 60,942.48, up 0.8%, and Seoul's Kospi at 5,375.36, up 2.6%. US consumer prices rose 0.4% in October, above the 0.3% forecast, on energy, food and housing.
