Russia and Ukraine carried out a large prisoner exchange in Abu Dhabi on Wednesday, as US envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff prepared to travel to Moscow for talks, Reuters reported.

Brent crude rose 0.6% to $92.01 a barrel after attacks near the Strait of Hormuz, with the Washington Institute counting 37 vessels hit in October and 10 more in November, Reuters reported.

Congo's Ebola outbreak reached 11,042 cases and 5,387 deaths, the Bundibugyo strain now present in 8 of the country's 26 provinces, the Associated Press reported.

COP31 opened in Antalya with 102 heads of state attending, as UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres warned that the window to limit global warming is closing fast, Reuters reported.

US consumer prices rose 0.4% in October, above the 0.3% forecast, while core prices rose 0.3% against a 0.2% forecast, the last inflation reading before the Federal Reserve meets on December 8-9, the Bureau of Labor Statistics reported.

The US House voted 211-204 to send articles of impeachment against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to committee, with Representative Thomas Massie the only Republican voting against the move, NPR reported.

In Seongnam, South Korea, a bus ploughed into 32 vehicles, injuring 57 people, nine of them seriously, after its brakes failed, Yonhap reported.
